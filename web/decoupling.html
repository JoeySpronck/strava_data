Aerobic decoupling

Drop in a run exported from Strava, drag two intervals over it, and see how much efficiency drifted between them. Efficiency factor is normalized graded pace divided by heart rate: on an honest aerobic effort it barely moves, and as aerobic fitness runs out the same pace starts costing more heartbeats. Decoupling is that drop, so lower is better — under about 5% is the usual marker for an aerobically sound long run.
